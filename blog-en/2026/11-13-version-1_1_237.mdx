## Allgemein

**Preview Word attachments (.docx & .doc):** You can now view attached Word documents directly in your browser without having to download them first. Clicking a Word file in the connected files list or selecting **Preview** from the **Actions** dropdown menu opens the document in a read-only viewer modal. The modal also provides a direct **Download** button in the footer for quick access whenever you need the file locally.
