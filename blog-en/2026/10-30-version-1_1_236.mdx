## Dashboard & Reports

### Idea management

Two new charts have been added to the idea management reporting.

The **Number of active ideas per person** chart shows how many active ideas each person is currently responsible for, broken down by their current status. Choose the role (creator, idea coordinator, idea manager or reviewer) and use **Older than** to show only ideas that have been waiting for at least a given number of days.
This shows you at a glance who has ideas piling up and where ideas have been waiting too long, so you can follow up or redistribute work before submissions stall.

![Number of active ideas per person chart](https://caqadmin.blob.core.windows.net/public-screenshots/manual-screenshots/number-of-active-ideas-per-person-chart.png)

The **Longest active idea per person** chart shows, for each person, how many days their longest active idea has been waiting in its current status, so ideas that have been stuck for a long time stand out quickly. The role selection and the **Older than** filter work the same way as in the **Number of active ideas per person** chart.

![Longest active idea per person chart](https://caqadmin.blob.core.windows.net/public-screenshots/manual-screenshots/longest-active-idea-per-person-chart.png)

**Improved user experience:** The charts in the idea management reporting are now easier to read. An info icon on each chart explains what its statuses mean for the selected role.

![Info tooltip explaining the statuses of a chart](https://caqadmin.blob.core.windows.net/public-screenshots/manual-screenshots/info-tooltip-explaining-the-statuses-of-a-chart.png)

People without any ideas are no longer listed, and you can show or hide a status by clicking its legend entry. The status colors have also been reworked: every status now has its own softer, clearly distinguishable color, so statuses such as approved and completed can be told apart at a glance. The workload charts now follow the order of the idea workflow.

![Workload charts in the order of the idea workflow](https://caqadmin.blob.core.windows.net/public-screenshots/manual-screenshots/workload-charts-in-the-order-of-the-idea-workflow.png)
