🚀 New update available! We have further optimized our system for you: Look forward to the new, clearer layout for detail pages in Tasks & Qualifications, the completion of our redesign for all selection fields including quick access to recently used entries, and more transparency for returned ideas in Idea Management.
{/* truncate */}

## General

- **Selection field redesign completed:** All selection fields in qmBase now share the new, consistent design. In addition, your recently selected entries are shown at the top of the list under **Recently selected**, so values you use often are just one click away.

  ![Selection field with recently selected entries](https://caqadmin.blob.core.windows.net/public-screenshots/manual-screenshots/selection-field-recently-selected.png)

- **Further selection field improvements:** Fields that allow several entries are now labeled in the plural, and search in selection fields and lists works more consistently.

## Company functions & Qualifications

### New detail page for tasks & qualifications

The task and qualification detail pages now feature a new, clearer layout. These are the first pages in the new design; further areas of qmBase will follow step by step.

![Qualification detail page](https://caqadmin.blob.core.windows.net/public-screenshots/manual-screenshots/detailseite-qualifikation.png)

**New header section with key data:** Previously, the most important details about a qualification were spread across different parts of the page. You can now find them gathered at the top in the newly designed header section.
Below the title, key data is clearly organized in a table: the responsible person, tags, as well as qualification-specific details such as monitoring interval, warning lead time, and color.

**Self-assignment & verification proofs bundled:** Settings for self-assignment and verification proofs (documents, forms & quizzes, training) along with self-assignment rules are now organized in a dedicated area with collapsible sections.
Each section indicates whether it is active and how many entries are configured even when collapsed.

## Idea management

**Returned ideas:** An idea becomes visible to the other people involved as soon as its creator submits it.
From then on, it also stays visible when it is returned to the creator: ideas with the status **Returned to sender** can now be opened by contributors, idea coordinators, idea managers and administrators, so everyone involved can follow the idea while it is being revised.
Only the creator can edit the idea, and drafts that have not been submitted yet remain visible only to the creator.
