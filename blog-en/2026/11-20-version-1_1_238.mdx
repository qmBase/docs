## Allgemein

- **Selection field redesign completed:** All selection fields in qmBase now share the new, consistent design. In addition, your recently selected entries are shown at the top of the list under **Recently selected**, so values you use often are just one click away.

  ![Selection field with recently selected entries](https://caqadmin.blob.core.windows.net/public-screenshots/manual-screenshots/selection-field-recently-selected.png)

- **Further selection field improvements:** Fields that allow several entries are now labeled in the plural, and search in selection fields and lists works more consistently.
- **Returned ideas:** An idea becomes visible to the other people involved as soon as its creator submits it. From then on, it also stays visible when it is returned to the creator: ideas with the status **Returned to sender** can now be opened by contributors, idea coordinators, idea managers and administrators, so everyone involved can follow the idea while it is being revised. Only the creator can edit the idea, and drafts that have not been submitted yet remain visible only to the creator.

## Dashboard & Reports

### Idea management

**Open ideas directly from the charts:** In charts that show one idea per person, such as **Longest active idea per person**, you can now click a bar to open the corresponding idea. This lets you jump straight from an idea that has been waiting too long to its details and follow up immediately.

**Status explanations (fixed):** The info icons on the idea management charts now explain the statuses correctly for the selected role.

**Employee tables (fixed):** The layout of the six employee tables on the idea management reporting page has been corrected, so the tables are displayed consistently and are easier to read.
